Molly Given is a journalist and features editor with over a decade of experience, covering arts, entertainment, lifestyle, and culture, with a focus on emerging trends and in-depth interviews across film, literature, and television.

Given is currently a features writer for Metro Philadelphia Newspaper, and has previously contributed to Fodor’s Travel, AM Metro New York, YourTango, as well as other Metro publications worldwide. Her work has earned industry recognition, including First Place honors from the Pennsylvania NewsMedia Association’s Keystone Media Awards.
In addition to her journalism career, Given is expanding into long-form creative writing and is currently working on her first novel — a story where ‘Big Little Lies’ meets ‘Baywatch.’
